## Who I Am

I'm **Khalid Murtala**, founder of **[The Web3 Wizard](https://www.theweb3wizard.xyz)** — a founder-led AI-native Web3 product studio based in Kano, Nigeria.

I build focused products that solve real problems at the intersection of **AI, Web3, Solana, and automation**. My process is direct:

> **Bring me the problem. I'll turn it into a product.**

No inflated claims. No generic agency work. Just focused MVPs and working product capabilities built with AI-native execution and honest scope.

|---|---|

- Start with the real problem, not the technology
- Build the smallest useful product, not the largest possible system
- AI accelerates the work — human judgment directs it
- Every limitation is documented, not hidden

## Selected Work

### 🤖 [Valor](https://github.com/theweb3wizard/Valor) — AI Agent for Telegram
An autonomous AI agent that lives inside Telegram. Evaluates community messages for contribution quality using Gemini AI and auto-tips high-quality contributors in real USDT on-chain via WDK wallets — no human in the loop.

`Next.js` `Gemini AI` `Tether WDK` `Telegram Bot API` `TypeScript`

[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/LIVE-valor--tgbot.vercel.app-D7A84B?style=flat-square)](https://valor-tgbot.vercel.app)

### 🔍 [WalletLens](https://github.com/theweb3wizard/walletlens) — AI-Powered EVM Wallet Intelligence
AI-powered wallet intelligence for EVM chains. Paste any wallet address on Ethereum, Polygon, BNB, Arbitrum, or Base — get behavioral analysis, portfolio breakdown, risk signals, and transaction history in plain English.

`Next.js` `Groq AI` `Moralis` `Supabase` `TypeScript`

[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/LIVE-walletlens--hq.vercel.app-78A9FF?style=flat-square)](https://walletlens-hq.vercel.app)

`Next.js` `OpenAI API` `TypeScript`

[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/LIVE-write3--ai.vercel.app-69C98B?style=flat-square)](https://write3-ai.vercel.app)

`TypeScript` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Vercel`

`Next.js` `Gemini AI` `Injective SDK` `TypeScript`

[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/LIVE-orderflow--hq.vercel.app-EF7777?style=flat-square)](https://orderflow-hq.vercel.app)

### 🐰 [TaxRabbit](https://taxrabbit.xyz) — Crypto Tax Cost Basis Tool
Solves the blank cost basis problem on IRS Form 1099-DA — the silent issue causing thousands of dollars in crypto tax overpayment for people who don't know the blank box is their responsibility to fill.

`Next.js` `TypeScript`

[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/LIVE-taxrabbit.xyz-F59E0B?style=flat-square)](https://taxrabbit.xyz)

- **Product Discovery Sprint** — turn a problem or idea into a build-ready product direction
