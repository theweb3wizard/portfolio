## How Pricing Works
- **Model:** Scoped fixed fee per engagement, agreed in writing before work begins. No hourly billing, no open-ended scope.
- **Payment:** 50% upfront, 50% on handover (or milestones for larger builds). Invoicing via agreed method.
- **Revisions:** One round of scope-aligned refinements within agreed deliverables. Scope expansion = new engagement.
- **Handover:** Deployed, documented, with known limitations. No ongoing maintenance included unless separately agreed.
